# Caeliscope Partners, Community & Recognition

English · [Español](README.es.md)

*An independent recognition directory by Hermetica Labs.*

Caeliscope is built through independent research, technical development, testing and the visibility that people and projects give it. This directory records, with verifiable references, who has contributed and how.

## Categories

| Category | What it recognises |
|---|---|
| [Partners](PARTNERS/README.md) | Organizations, projects or people with an agreed collaboration. |
| [Testers](TESTERS/README.md) | People who test Caeliscope or Caelis Engine, report problems and give technical feedback. |
| [Contributors](CONTRIBUTORS/README.md) | Dissemination, articles, reviews, documentation, references and technical contributions. |
| [Research & Corpus](RESEARCH/README.md) | Inclusion in research corpora and knowledge infrastructures, such as the XERJ Corpus Hub. |

A person or project can appear in more than one category; each type of contribution is named explicitly.

## Rules

- **Issued only by Hermetica Labs.** The profiles live in this repository, on the `main` branch.
- **Verifiable identity and evidence.** Every entry links to a public reference.
- **Consent.** Biographical data or photographs are published only with the person's authorisation.
- **No endorsement implied.** A badge recognises a documented contribution. It is not a technical certification, a commercial approval, or an endorsement by the recognised party of the whole ecosystem.
- **"Since" date.** The profile of every partner, tester or contributor shows, next to its relationship with Caeliscope, a box with the date it began (`since YYYY-MM-DD`). Research & Corpus entries do not carry it.
- **Contribution ≠ partnership ≠ external recognition.** An editorial mention is not presented as a commercial alliance, and inclusion in an external corpus is not presented as a contribution to Caeliscope.

## Official badge

Partners and testers can copy the badge from their own profile and place it on their website, GitHub or portfolio. It always links back to their profile in this directory.
