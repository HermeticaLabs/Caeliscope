# Profile template

English · [Español](TEMPLATE.es.md)

Use this structure for every new partner, tester or contributor. Copy it to `PARTNERS/`, `TESTERS/` or `CONTRIBUTORS/` as `name-in-kebab-case.md` (and `.es.md`), and add the entry to the category index and to `README.md`.

```markdown
# Name

English · [Español](name.es.md)

**Relationship with Caeliscope** <kbd><b>since YYYY-MM-DD</b></kbd>

[![badge](badge-url)](profile-url)

## About
## Relationship with Caeliscope
## Social networks
## Recognition
## Use your badge
```

- The `since` box always sits right after the relationship name, on the same line. It is required for partners, testers and contributors, and not used in Research & Corpus.
- Publish names, photographs or biographical data only with the person's consent.
- Describe only what can be verified with a public reference.
