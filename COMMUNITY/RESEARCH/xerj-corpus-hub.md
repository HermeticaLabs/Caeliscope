# XERJ Corpus Hub

**Organization:** Hermetica Labs · **Project:** Caeliscope · **Type:** Research infrastructure & corpus inclusion

## About

[The XERJ Corpus Hub](https://hub.xerj.org/) is a shared reference corpus infrastructure for AI agents. It catalogues open-source repositories so they can be found and cited with their attribution.

Hermetica Labs takes part as a contributor, and the moon plugins of Caeliscope are selected there as a reference implementation (see the [moon plugins documentation](https://hermeticalabs.github.io/Caeliscope/docs/en/plugins-lunas.html)).

## References

- [Hermetica Labs on the XERJ Corpus Hub](https://hub.xerj.org/contributors/HermeticaLabs)

## Scope

Inclusion documents that the repository belongs to that corpus. It does not mean that XERJ has certified the astronomical calculations of Caelis Engine, and it is not a Caeliscope partner badge.
