## About

[El Gran Observatorio](https://elgranobservatorio.com/) is an independent Spanish-language publication on astronomy, stargazing and optical equipment (telescopes and binoculars), with educational content and technical analysis.

## Relationship with Caeliscope

| | |
|---|---|
| **Roles** | Partner · Official Beta Tester · Media & Editorial Outreach |
| **Contribution** | Testing of Caeliscope and editorial coverage of the project for the astronomy community. |
