## Globe and sky

Caeliscope has two main views:

- **Globe.** Choose the observer's place. It shows that place's local time and the UTC time. Press **My location** to use your device's (the browser will ask for permission; the location never leaves your device).
- **Sky.** What an observer at that place sees looking up, with horizon, optional landscape and every object at its real position.

Press `L` at any time to go back to the globe and change place.

## Projections

In **Options → Projection** you can choose how the celestial dome is drawn:

| Projection | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Stereographic (immersive) | The default: natural, without distorting shapes. |
| Equidistant (fisheye) | Shows almost the whole sky at once, with uniform angular scale. |
| Perspective (camera) | Like a photographic camera: ideal with a narrow field of view. |

## The time bar

The bottom bar controls the instant being shown:

- **Speed arrows.** Move forward or back 30 s, 1 min or 10 min per real second.
- **Real.** Returns to the real clock. **Now** also goes back to the current instant.
- **Pause.** `Space` stops or resumes the flow.
- **Every unit is editable.** With the arrows next to the year, month, day, hour, minutes and seconds you can raise or lower that unit.
- **Calendar.** Opens a local date and time picker to jump to an exact moment.
- **Hour of day.** Marks whether it is day, dawn, dusk or night at the chosen place.
- **Find event.** Jumps to the next or previous eclipse, new Moon, equinox or solstice. See [Events and eclipses](eventos-y-eclipses.md).

The clock always shows the local time of the chosen place and its UTC equivalent.

## Full screen and night mode

`F` turns on full screen. `N` applies a red filter that protects dark-adapted vision, useful if you use Caeliscope next to a telescope.
